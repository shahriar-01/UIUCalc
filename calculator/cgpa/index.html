How it works — GPA & CGPA formulas

GPA — one trimester

Σ (Grade Point × Credit Hours) Σ Credit Hours
  1. Convert each course's percentage or letter grade to its grade point.
  2. Multiply the grade point by the course's credit hours.
  3. Add up all the results.
  4. Divide the total by the sum of all credit hours.

CGPA — across trimesters

Σ (GPA₁ × Credits₁ + GPA₂ × Credits₂ + …) Total Credits
  1. Calculate each trimester's GPA with the formula on the left.
  2. Multiply each trimester's GPA by that trimester's credit hours.
  3. Add up all the results.
  4. Divide by the sum of all credit hours from all trimesters.

Displayed values round to 2 decimals; the underlying math never rounds intermediate steps.
