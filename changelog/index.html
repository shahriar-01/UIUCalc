Changelog

What's new in the toolkit.

v1.1.0 latest

2026-10-16
  • Changed — New UC logo with separate light and dark variants that follow the active theme.
  • Changed — Fully fluid layout: tool pages, forms and tables now adapt to the space available (including the sidebar state) instead of fixed breakpoints.
  • Fixed — Sidebar label and state now stay in sync after a resize; the hidden mobile drawer is no longer reachable by keyboard.
  • Fixed — Planner gauge turns red when a target is unreachable.

v1.0.0

2026-10-15
  • Added — CGPA Calculator with new-course and retake handling, live results and the UIU grade scale (exportable as image/CSV).
  • Added — Tuition Fee Calculator: per-credit tuition, trimester fee, waiver/scholarship rules, stackable special waivers, FYDP & retake policies and the 40-30-30 installment plan with a copyable summary.
  • Added — Target CGPA Planner with next-trimester, multi-trimester, until-graduation and what-if scenarios, gauge and grade-mix suggestions.
  • Added — Exam Conflict Tracker fed by an editable /data/exam-schedule.json: critical same-slot clashes and same-day pressure warnings on a day-by-slot grid.
  • Added — Light/dark themes, collapsible sidebar with remembered state, per-tool "How to use" dialogs, personalized greeting and saved inputs.
  • Added — Unit tests for every calculation engine (npm test).
